We have built a digital triage system in which an AI chatbot collects patient-reported symptom text through a messaging interface and combines it with heart-rate variability (HRV) data streamed from a consumer wearable device to compute a same-day urgency score for primary care patients. Patients above a threshold score are automatically flagged for same-day telehealth visits instead of the standard next-available-appointment queue.

The novel part, from our perspective, is fusing free-text symptom input with continuous HRV time-series data in a single triage model, rather than using symptom checklists alone or wearable vitals alone. We want to know whether combining conversational symptom intake with wearable HRV data for same-day primary-care triage prioritization has already been described or patented.
