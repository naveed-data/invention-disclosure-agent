We are proposing a closed-loop insulin delivery system that pairs a continuous glucose monitor (CGM) with an insulin pump, where dosing decisions are made by a reinforcement-learning agent that is continuously retrained on each individual patient's glucose response over time, rather than using a fixed PID or model-predictive-control algorithm.

The reinforcement-learning agent's reward function penalizes both hypoglycemic and hyperglycemic excursions and is updated nightly using that day's glucose trace, with the goal of personalizing dosing behavior faster than existing adaptive algorithms. We want to know whether an RL-based, continuously self-retraining dosing algorithm for closed-loop insulin delivery has already been disclosed, separate from the well-established CGM+pump hardware combination itself.
