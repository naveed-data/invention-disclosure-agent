We have designed a biodegradable, single-use microneedle patch that delivers a patient-specific consortium of three engineered probiotic bacterial strains directly into the dermis, with sequential release of each strain staggered over 72 hours. Release timing is controlled by a photonic, pH-responsive hydrogel layer that visibly changes color as each stage releases, allowing a caregiver to visually confirm dosing progress without any electronics.

The patch is designed specifically for a named ultra-rare pediatric skin disorder with an estimated incidence of fewer than 1 in 1,000,000 births, where the engineered probiotic consortium is intended to locally modulate skin microbiome composition. We want to know whether this specific combination -- staggered engineered-probiotic delivery via a color-changing photonic pH-responsive microneedle patch for this rare disorder -- has any close prior art.
